about

I am currently a Ph.D. student at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ), advised by Professor Zhuotao Tian. I received my M.E. degree from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I was supervised by Professor Junwei Han, IEEE Fellow, and my B.E. degree from Sichuan University.

My research interests lie in multimodal large models and intelligent agents (AI Agents), with a focus on building systems that can perceive, reason, and act across diverse modalities. I am also broadly interested in deep learning, computer vision, and AIGC. I hope to contribute meaningful work to these rapidly evolving fields in the future.
