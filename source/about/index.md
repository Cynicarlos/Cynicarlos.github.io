I am currently a Ph.D. student at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ), advised by Professor [Zhuotao Tian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mEjhz-IAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I received my M.E. degree from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I was supervised by Professor [Junwei Han](https://scholar.google.ae/citations?user=xrqsoesAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN), IEEE Fellow, and my B.E. degree from Sichuan University.

My research interests lie in **multimodal large models** and **intelligent agents (AI Agents)**, with a focus on building systems that can perceive, reason, and act across diverse modalities. I am also broadly interested in deep learning, computer vision, and AIGC. I hope to contribute meaningful work to these rapidly evolving fields in the future.
